## Mission and scope
Kymata Labs makes open-source AI tools, models and infrastructure easier to understand through focused, source-backed indexes. We publish dated source signals, explain ranking methods and limitations, and separate advertising from ranking and inclusion.

Explore developer repositories and the public activity around them.

## Supported capabilities
- Browse dated catalog records and published detail pages
- Search the catalog locally in the browser; raw search text is not sent by these controls.
- Use the available catalog category filters and sorting controls
- Read source references, snapshot dates, methodology, selection guidance and evidence limits.

## Evidence limits
- Snapshot signals are not proof of quality, safety, license suitability or fitness for a project. Verify upstream sources before adoption.
- Search and filters are browser controls; this description does not advertise a remote search API or an MCP endpoint.
- Crawl permission and machine-readable descriptions do not guarantee crawling, indexing, ranking or AI citation.
